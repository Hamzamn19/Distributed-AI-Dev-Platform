# Sprint 4 Planning — Distributed AI Dev Platform

Dates: 25–26 November 2026, **ending today (26 November) with the Sprint Review** — a compressed pull-based sprint opened right after the Sprint 3 merge; by the team's week accounting this is the end of Sprint 4 · Owner: Hamza (Scrum Master) · Report: [report.md](report.md)

## Sprint Goal

Make the platform usable and observable in real use: the user controls a running
project (cancel, retry, queue, export, follow-up change), sees the model work
live, and the generated applications stop looking identical — one visual style
per project — while the team's `main` line stays green.

## Planning basis

Sprint 4 was not planned from the board up front: it opened as a **pull-based
sprint** — every card was pulled from the findings of the live testing of
25 November (the Sprint 3 closing week). This is recorded openly because it is
the interesting Scrum fact of the sprint: the backlog was generated by what the
real model exposed when it was actually used, not by estimation up front.
